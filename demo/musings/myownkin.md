For so long I have kept myself all down under.<br> 
Quelling that hunger, I learned in small portions. <br> Deepened fasts, righteousness, nauseous to change. 

When I got that bite of you, I knew I needed more.<br>Obsessive like I—

<em>You</em> told me to go deeper. . .

And what that beast inside of my chest would do to you, is your want. <br>
You say this season's harvest is bountiful. That I should come inside, <br>I had been playing in the garden dirt for too long..

Our feast is your undoing, and that gnawing of you is just growing more, but now. I know not only of your lifeblooded, lilac scents. But why you chase the drawn out nature of pleasure that has been each of our safeties.

If I save it for tomorrow, <br>If it —— 
<br>I can just stay still. No.

I want you now.<br>
And you still tell me to go deeper.<br>
So I do.<br>
And I count down from ten.

Still having to start over again —— from ten. It became twenty. A minute, two. After hours, a day passed by. Five of them. A whole week, next. Fifty-two of them all —— a year.  My longest was five? It had been several...

And when I finally break, and that dirt is calling my name again. <br>Back at the Garden, underneath now.<br>
You asked me, "Why isn't the hole deeper?" <br>That six feet is still "not enough to fit my depth."

You dug the rest, and —— <br>
Asked if you would take me with you. <br>There would be room for two, now.

Naively or not, you jumped first. <br>Who am I to dissuade the trust you own?<br>
You push your fingers against my lips, and in an audible, hushed coo. 

My doubts seem to sink away...yours meet mine. <br>The dirt caves over, and I'm reminded of the first time an I love you was muttered.<br>
One we still fight over for who said what first, <br>
Now... I guess that's what eternity is.

Being able to have the time to recount all the moments, all the reasons, off all the basis of who said what, 'first'.<br>
And still, with naivety and all. 

It's been yours to say from the start, if it were up to me.
